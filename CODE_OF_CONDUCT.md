# Code of conduct

GovernCode is about the work: code, designs and ideas. Everyone is welcome here, whatever tools,
operating systems or AI models they love, use, or avoid.

## Respect, always

- **Talk about the work, not the person.** Critique a design, a pull request or an idea as hard as
  you like. Never attack, mock or belittle the people behind them.
- **Leave other choices alone.** Love AI or distrust it, prefer one operating system, editor or
  project over another: all fine, and none of it needs defending here. Don't start or feed
  arguments about which tools or people are "bad".
- **Assume good faith.** Ask before you accuse; explain before you judge. People are learning,
  and many are writing in a second language.
- **No harassment, ever.** That includes insults, slurs, threats, sexual content, unwelcome
  attention, sharing someone's private information, and deliberately derailing a discussion.

## Scope

Everywhere GovernCode lives: its repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere
you speak for the project.

## If something is wrong

Email **conduct@governcode.com**. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept private. We may
remove comments, lock threads, or block people who break these rules, and we will tell the person
reporting what we did.

Security problems are different: please report them privately as described in each repository's
`SECURITY.md`.
